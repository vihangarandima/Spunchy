# Spunchy staff guide: updating the menu

This guide is for the counter team. You don't need any technical knowledge.
Everything you change shows up on the public website within about a second.

## 1. Open the staff page

On your phone or the counter computer, go to:

**`<your website>/admin`**

Bookmark it or add it to your home screen.

## 2. Sign in (first time on a device)

1. Type your staff email address.
2. Tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open your email and tap the link. The staff page opens and you're signed in.

No password is needed. If you get no email, ask the manager to check that your
email is on the staff list.

## 3. Change a price

1. Find the item. Scroll, or type its name in **Find an item…**.
2. Tap the **Price (Rs.)** box and type the new price in whole rupees (e.g. `300`).
3. Tap anywhere else, or press **Enter**.
4. You'll see **Saving…** and then **Saved ✓**. Done.

Leave the price box empty to show "Ask in store" on the website.

## 4. Update how many are left

Use the **−** and **+** buttons next to **Stock**. Each tap saves straight away.
When stock reaches 0, the website shows **Sold out**.

## 5. Mark something sold out (or back in stock)

- Tap **Mark sold out**. The row turns pink and the website shows "Sold out".
- When it's back, tap **Back in stock**.

## 6. Check it worked

Open the website on another phone and look at the **Menu**. The change appears
within a second, and the card briefly glows yellow.

## Good to know

- You can only change **price, stock and sold out**. Names, photos and new
  items are added by the developer. Send the details to them.
- If you see a red error message, check your internet connection and try again.
- Tap **Sign out** at the top when you're done on a shared device.
